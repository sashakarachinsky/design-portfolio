About the logo

The point is that the logo is actually a circle. Zero, nothing, emptiness, a black hole. I had nothing left to do but take the plunge. To my surprise I could not find a single just-a-circle logo for the whole week. As a result, it became not only the service symbol, but also the main function key. Unfortunately, only in the release beta-version.
