True Czech Chandeliers Logo

A logo with a twist—text style using the characteristic Czech alphabet «č with gachek».

A little later it was decided to add a simplified coat of arms of the Czech Republic.

Simple and tastefull Inspiration—coat of arms and č

Gachek (Czech háček—hook)—a diacritic mark placed in the Latin alphabet over some letters to give them a new sound value.

Source—Wikipedia .

The very first version of—č not in the best place.
