My dream came true—I've created my first font. Please welcome Neo Hippie. By the way, you can download it for free.

Surely you've seen a lot of similar letters; I'm just like that! Sometimes you walk down the street and suddenly there's a poster, a sign or, for example, a cafe menu. And a single letter suddenly gives an inexpressible feeling. You rejoice at its beauty.

So I've decided to xollect these letters, redesign them and make a font.
